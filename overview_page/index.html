⚠️ Important Notice

  • Exhibitor badges are strictly reserved for exhibitors and exhibiting team members
  • Should not be transferred or "lent" to non-exhibiting personnel/visitors
  • Any visiting staff should register as visitors
  • No requests for exhibitor badges for persons under the age of 21 years old
  • Only permits access during build-up and show opening days
  • Badges must be worn at all times - strict security will be maintained at the venue
  • Report lost badges to organiser's office for duplicate issuance
